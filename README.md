  <h3>En Garde is a mod that makes Minecraft's PVP system more fun while not straying too far from the vanilla PVP mechanics.</h3>

## Features
### Parry
The main feature of this mod is being able to parry attacks:
* While holding a weapon, if you press the keybind (set to R by default), you are now in the blocking stance.
* If another player attacks you while you are parrying, the attack is blocked and your weapon goes on cooldown and you can't parry with it for a set duration.

### Weapon attributes
* The range of some weapons has been modified to add more variety to different weapon combinations.
* The speed of some weapons has been changed.
* There is a new attribute called shield size which sets how big of an area your parry blocks.
* There is another new attribute called shield cooldown which determined how long your parry goes on cooldown for after blocking an attack.

All of these attributes are changeable with datapacks.
  
### Weapon reworks

**Mace rework**  
The vanilla mace is a bit overpowered, so this mod reworks its mechanics:
* The mace's scaled damage now maxes out at the height of 2.5 blocks. 
* The mace's base damage and range has been increased to make it a more viable weapon on its own for PVP, even without slam attacks.
* The Mace is now a heavy item, meaning that you hold with 2 hands and can't use your offhand while holding a mace, but when you parry an attack you stun your attacker.

**Spear rework**  
As cool as the spear is in Minecraft, I don't think it fits well into the game, so its functionality has been modified:
* The jab and spear attacks have been replaced by the regular Minecraft attack, with a long range.
* The spear is now throwable like a trident, dealing high damage as a projectile.

**Bow rework**  
The bow has been reworked slightly to make shooting a bow more realistic and fun:
* When you right click, instead of the string automatically drawing back like in vanilla, you actually have to scroll your mouse to pull it back.
* Shots from bows deal a lot more damage if you pull the bow far enough.
